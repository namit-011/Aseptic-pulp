Why Aseptic Processing?

Aseptic processing is the gold standard for preserving fruit pulp at its nutritional peak — no heat degradation, no cold chain dependency.

Nutritional Integrity

Ultra-high-temperature processing eliminates pathogens while retaining vitamins, colour, and natural flavour compounds intact. No compromise on what nature put in.

98%Vitamin C retention
100%Natural colour preserved
0Nutrient loss in transit

18-Month Shelf Life

Stored at ambient temperature — no refrigeration required until opening. Dramatically reduces supply-chain costs and wastage, and opens global export markets.

0℉Cold chain needed
~40%Lower logistics cost

Zero Additives

Pure fruit, nothing else. No preservatives, artificial colours, or sweeteners. Label-clean product ready for premium branding and health-conscious consumers.

Transparency

Complete Farm-to-Drum
Traceability

Every batch of InnoFarms aseptic pulp is fully traceable — from the specific orchard block and harvest date to the processing batch number on your drum.

01

Orchard Identification

Each fruit consignment is tagged with GPS-linked farm block ID, farmer details, and harvest date.

02

Farm-Gate Processing

Pulping and aseptic filling happens within hours of harvest at our on-farm processing unit — no transit loss of freshness.

03

Batch QC & Lab Testing

Every batch is tested for Brix, pH, microbial load, heavy metals, and pesticide residues before dispatch.

04

QR-Coded Drums

Scan the QR code on any drum to access the complete batch record — farm, farmer, harvest date, and all lab reports.

Our Story

Grown Across India,
Sealed with Science

InnoFarms Aseptic Pulp is the industrial-supply arm of InnoFarms — a farm-to-shelf agri company with deep roots across India. We partner directly with small-holder farmers spanning diverse agri-belts — from Kashmir orchards to Tamil Nadu tropicals, and from Maharashtra's famous mango belts to Meghalaya's exotic hill fruits — sourcing each variety at peak ripeness.

Our pulp is made using InnoFarms' own aseptic processing machines stationed at different farm locations pan-India. This on-farm processing model eliminates the traditional cold chain entirely, preserving the natural character of each fruit and ensuring nothing is lost between orchard and your production line.
